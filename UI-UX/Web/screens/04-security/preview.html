01

Encrypted at rest

Saved passwords and tokens are encrypted before storage. Your account password is hashed.

02

Secure storage on the phone

Tokens live in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore — never in plain app settings.

03

Never shown in full on the web

Once saved, a token never goes back to your browser. To change it, paste a new one.

04

Short sessions

Sign-ins expire quickly and renew quietly. The website signs you out after 30 idle minutes.

05

Your permissions only

JobTrigger can do only what your token allows. It never reads Jenkins’ credential store.

06

Optional app lock

Face ID or fingerprint, so an unlocked phone still can’t open JobTrigger.
