Security

Built by people who guard tokens for a living.

The app talks to your CI directly — never through us. Saved tokens are encrypted at rest and kept in your phone's secure storage.

How we handle your tokens →
Encrypted at rest Tokens are encrypted before they are stored.
Secure storage iOS Keychain and Android Keystore on the phone.
Masked on the web Saved tokens are never sent back to your browser.
Direct to your CI No JobTrigger proxy between you and Jenkins.
