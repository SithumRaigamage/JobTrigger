Encrypted at rest

Saved passwords and tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Your account password is hashed, never stored as text.

Secure storage on the phone

On the phone, tokens live in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore — never in plain app settings.

Never shown in full on the web

Once saved, a token is never sent back to your browser. To change it, you paste a new one.

Short sessions

Sign-ins expire quickly and renew quietly. The website signs you out after 30 minutes idle, and you can sign out of every device at once.

Your permissions, nothing more

JobTrigger can only do what your own token allows on your server. It never reads Jenkins' credential store.

Optional app lock

Turn on Face ID or fingerprint so someone holding your unlocked phone still can't open JobTrigger.

Our advice: give it the smallest token you can

  • Create a dedicated API token just for JobTrigger, so you can revoke it alone.
  • On GitHub, use a fine-grained token limited to the repositories you need.
  • Set an expiry date, and rotate it from the website when it runs out.
