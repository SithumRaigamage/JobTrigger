JobTrigger is run by [COMPANY NAME]. This policy explains what we keep about you when you use the JobTrigger app and website, and what we never see.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a hashed version of your password.
  • Your saved servers: names, URLs, usernames, and the tokens you add — encrypted before storage.
  • Which server is active for each tool.

What we don't see

  • Your builds, logs, jobs or code. The app talks to your CI servers directly.
  • Analytics or crash reports. The app has none today. If that changes, this policy changes first.
  • Advertising identifiers. We don't sell or share your data.

How it's used

Only to sign you in and hand your saved servers to your own phone. Build notifications are created on your phone, not sent by us.
